OUR NEW HOME · FRANKFURT’S OSTEND

Welcome to the Union-Areal 💜

A city within a city in the heart of Frankfurt’s Ostend. At the Union-Areal, the historic Union brewery meets modern loft architecture. Fifteen buildings, each with its own character, surround a piazza shaded by old chestnut trees.

Culture, dining and creative spaces bring people together here. It’s also the new home of our ALMA community, where we dance, learn and celebrate together.
