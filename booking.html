Your First Visit

First time at ALMA?

A trial class is the best way to get to know our teaching style, experience our community, and find the right class for you.

Important Information

  • Advance Registration Required: Spots are limited, please book your session online in advance.
  • Timing: Available during weeks 1 & 2 of our 6-week course cycle.
  • Select Your Level: If you are complete beginner, choose a Fundamentals/Beginner class. Prior experience is required for intermediate and advanced levels.
  • No Partner Needed: You are welcome to register and join on your own.
  • What to Wear: Comfortable clothing and clean shoes that allow for smooth turns.

Price: €10 per person (free upon full course sign-up or leaving a positive review)

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining us.

No. You can register and come by yourself. In our partner-dance classes we rotate partners, which gives everyone the opportunity to practise with different people and meet other dancers.
Wear comfortable clothes that allow you to move freely. We recommend clean indoor shoes with relatively smooth soles. Dance shoes are welcome but are not necessary for your first class.
If you have never danced before, start with one of our Fundamentals classes. If you already have Salsa or Bachata experience, choose a trial class at an appropriate level. Our teachers can then help you determine which class fits your current skills best.
Take our Salsa & Bachata Self-Assessment →
Our regular programme runs in course cycles. For complete beginners, we recommend starting at the beginning of a cycle so that you can build your fundamentals step by step. Trial-class availability is normally limited to the first two weeks of each cycle. Dancers with previous experience may be able to join intermediate or advanced classes later depending on level and availability.
If you enjoy the class and want to continue, you can choose the membership option that fits your training goals and the number of classes you would like to attend. Our team can also help you choose the right level and programme.
